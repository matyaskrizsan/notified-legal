Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Notified works entirely on-device - your topics and checklists are tied to your device, not a login.

How do I change my subscription or cancel?
Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. Go to Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions on your iPhone to view, change, or cancel.

What happens if I downgrade my plan?
Nothing is deleted. Topics and checklist items beyond your new plan's limit are paused (not checked, shown greyed out) until you upgrade again or free up a slot.

Why didn't I get notified about something?
Notified only sends a notification when it finds a genuine, recent match against one of your checklist items on your topic's schedule - not every mention of a keyword. Check the topic's News tab to see everything it's found.
